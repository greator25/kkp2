## Image Specifications
- **Logo**: PNG format, transparent background, 200x80px recommended
- **CEO Photo**: JPG format, 300x400px, professional portrait
- **Vehicle Images**: JPG format, 400x300px, high quality vehicle photos
- **Video Poster**: JPG format, 600x400px, company branding
- **Background Pattern**: JPG format, 200x200px, subtle Indian motif

## Notes
- All images should be optimized for web use
- Vehicle images should show the actual trucks from your fleet
- Background pattern should be subtle and not interfere with text readability
- Company video should showcase your transportation services
